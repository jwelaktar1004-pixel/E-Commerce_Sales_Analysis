# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Objective
Analyze sales and profitability trends for an e-commerce store to identify top-performing products, categories, and customer segments, and provide actionable insights for better revenue and profit management.

## Dataset Overview
- **Records:** 9,994  
- **Columns:** 21  
- Key Features: Order info, Customer info, Product info, Sales info  

## Key Findings
- **Top Sales & Profit Drivers:** Technology and Office Supplies  
- **Most Profitable Segment:** Home Office  
- **High Revenue but Low Profit:** Consumer segment  
- **Seasonal Trends:** Peak sales in Q4 (Nov–Dec); mid-year dip (Apr–Jul)  

## Recommendations
- Focus on high-margin products  
- Improve low-margin or loss-making categories (e.g., Tables)  
- Implement targeted campaigns during slow months  
- Plan inventory and marketing around seasonal peaks  

## Conclusion
Optimizing high-margin products, managing low-performing categories, and aligning strategies with seasonal trends can improve profitability and stabilize sales.
